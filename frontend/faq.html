Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about orders, payments, refunds, accounts, and support.

AZIEL is a game top-up platform for fast and secure digital game purchases.
AZIEL currently focuses on Myanmar and Thailand users.
Myanmar users can use KBZPay, WavePay, and AYA Pay. Thailand users can use PromptPay and SCB when available.
Most orders are processed as quickly as possible after payment confirmation. Some orders may take longer during maintenance or high traffic.
You can check your order status from the Transactions page.
Refunds are available only under eligible conditions. Please read the Refund Policy for details.
Orders with incorrect User ID or Server ID may not be refundable after delivery. Always check your information before payment.
You can contact AZIEL Support through the Support Center or Live Chat.
