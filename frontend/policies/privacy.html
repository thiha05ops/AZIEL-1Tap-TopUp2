Privacy Policy

Last Updated : July 2026

Overview

AZIEL 1Tap Shop respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect your account and order data.

Information We Collect

  • Account name and email address.
  • Game User ID and Server ID for order delivery.
  • Order history, payment method and transaction status.
  • Support messages and communication records.

How We Use Your Data

  • To process game top-up orders.
  • To confirm payments and update order status.
  • To provide customer support.
  • To improve service quality and prevent fraud.

Payment Information

AZIEL never stores your bank password, card details or payment credentials. Payments are handled through supported providers or manual verification.

Data Protection

We use reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect your account and order information. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

If you have questions about privacy or your personal information, please contact AZIEL Support.
