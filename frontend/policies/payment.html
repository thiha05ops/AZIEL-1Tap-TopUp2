Accepted Payment Methods

  • 🇲🇲 WavePay
  • 🇲🇲 AYA Pay
  • 🇹🇭 PromptPay
  • 🇹🇭 SCB

Payment Confirmation

Orders begin processing only after successful payment confirmation. For supported payment methods, payment verification may occur automatically. Some payment methods may require manual verification.

Failed Payment

  • Payment not completed.
  • Payment timeout.
  • Incorrect transfer amount.
  • Bank or payment provider error.

Security

AZIEL does not store your payment credentials. All online payments are securely processed through supported payment providers.

Need Help?

If you experience payment issues, please prepare:

  • Order ID
  • Payment Method
  • Payment Time
  • Receipt (if available)
