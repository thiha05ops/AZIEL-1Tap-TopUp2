Payment Confirmation

Orders begin processing only after successful payment confirmation. Some payment methods are verified automatically while others may require manual verification.

Failed Payment

  • Payment not completed.
  • Payment timeout.
  • Incorrect transfer amount.
  • Bank or payment provider error.

Security

AZIEL never stores your payment credentials. Online payments are securely processed through supported payment providers.

Need Help?

If you experience payment issues, please prepare the following information.
