Refund Policy

Last Updated : July 2026

Overview

AZIEL 1Tap Shop provides digital game top-up services. Since digital goods may be delivered quickly after payment, refunds are only available under eligible conditions described below.

Eligible For Refund

  • ✅ Order failed after payment was confirmed.
  • ✅ Order was cancelled by AZIEL or supplier.
  • ✅ Payment was successful but top-up was not delivered.
  • ✅ Duplicate payment for the same order.
  • ✅ System error or supplier error confirmed by AZIEL Support.

Not Eligible

  • ❌ Wrong User ID entered.
  • ❌ Wrong Server ID entered.
  • ❌ Wrong game or package selected.
  • ❌ Customer changed their mind after payment.
  • ❌ Item has already been delivered successfully.
  • ❌ Third-party account restrictions or account problems.

Refund Process

  1. Customer can request a refund only for failed or cancelled orders.
  2. Customer must provide a clear refund reason.
  3. Admin reviews the order, payment status, and delivery status.
  4. If approved, the refund amount will be returned to the customer’s AZIEL Wallet.
  5. If rejected, the customer will see the rejection reason in tracking or notification.

Wallet Refund

Approved refunds are credited to your AZIEL Wallet. You can use the refunded balance for future purchases on AZIEL.

Review Time

Refund requests are reviewed as soon as possible. In most cases, review may take up to 24 hours depending on order details and payment verification.

Abuse Prevention

Fake refund requests, repeated abuse, or fraudulent claims may result in account restriction or suspension.

Need Help?

If you believe your order qualifies for a refund, please contact AZIEL Support or submit a refund request from your order tracking page.

  • Order ID
  • Payment Method
  • Payment Receipt or slip if available
  • Description of the issue
