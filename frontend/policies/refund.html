Refund Policy

Last Updated : June 2026

Overview

AZIEL 1Tap Shop provides digital game top-up services. Since digital goods are delivered instantly after payment, refunds are only available under specific conditions described below.

Eligible For Refund

  • ✅ Payment was successful but no order was created.
  • ✅ Duplicate payment for the same order.
  • ✅ Payment confirmed but delivery permanently failed due to our system.
  • ✅ AZIEL Support confirms the transaction cannot be completed.

Not Eligible

  • ❌ Wrong User ID entered.
  • ❌ Wrong Server ID entered.
  • ❌ Wrong game selected.
  • ❌ Customer changed their mind.
  • ❌ Item has already been delivered.
  • ❌ Third-party account problems.

Refund Processing

Approved refunds are normally processed within 1–7 business days depending on the payment provider.

Need Help?

If you believe your payment qualifies for a refund, please contact AZIEL Support with:

  • Order ID
  • Payment Method
  • Payment Receipt (if available)
  • Description of the issue
